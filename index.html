Retail Walk-in, Operations & Staff LMS Platform Overview

A dedicated in-store retail operations and workforce enablement application built with Flutter for showroom staff and retail managers. It solves in-store showroom walk-in tracking, instant barcode stock verification, daily operational task dispatch, and continuous staff product training.

The 3 In-Store Problems It Solves

Lost Walk-In Visitors

Showroom customers previously went unrecorded or were noted on paper slips that were lost. The app enables fast digital registration with interest categories, budget brackets, and consultation outcomes.

In-Store Inventory Friction

Sales associates had to leave customers on the sales floor to check product availability on back-office desktops. The camera barcode checker brings live stock and pricing directly to the sales floor.

Staff Product Knowledge Gaps

Frequent retail staff turnover caused inconsistent sales pitches and product confusion. Structured video onboarding modules and mandatory quizzes ensure certified catalog knowledge.

Technical Architecture & System Layers

Presentation Layer (UI / UX)Clean Architecture in Flutter 3.x; role-segmented dashboards (Admin vs. Staff), dynamic bottom navigation, custom task cards, and responsive Material 3 layouts.
State & Controller LayerScoped Provider architecture with lifecycle-aware disposal to isolate feature contexts (walk-ins, tasks, training modules) and prevent cross-screen data overrides.
Data & Service Layer (Offline-Tolerant)REST API client with automatic JWT token refresh, SharedPreferences repository caching for offline tolerance, and Firebase Remote Config.
Device & Hardware IntegrationsNative camera scanner for Barcode/QR decoding, GPS geolocation for store check-ins, and hardware-accelerated video player with progressive buffering.

Engineering Challenges Solved

Training State Collisions & Module Caching

Problem: Switching between multiple training modules caused Provider state collisions and progress resets.
Solution: Implemented isolated repository-level caching per module with lifecycle-aware disposal so sequential training progress is preserved offline.

Video UI Jank on Low-End Devices

Problem: High-definition training videos caused severe frame drops and stutter on budget Android store tablets.
Solution: Implemented progressive video stream chunking, texture-backed video controllers, and shimmer skeleton placeholders for smooth 60fps scrolling.

Instant In-Store Barcode Scanning

Problem: Slow autofocus and heavy scanner libraries delayed customer interaction on the sales floor.
Solution: Integrated lightweight camera scanner with continuous frame analysis and local SKU caching, fetching stock levels in under 1 second.

Rent Tracker Platform Architecture

An ultra-clean, cross-platform property management and cashflow tracking suite built with Flutter (Material 3), Riverpod 2.x, and Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security). Designed for landlords managing commercial stores, offices, and residential properties.

Clean Layered Architecture

Presentation Layer (UI & Widgets)DashboardScreen, RentListScreen, RentFormScreen; custom reusable widgets: RentCard, SummaryCard, StatusBadge, FilterBar, and animated empty states.
State Management & Business Logic (Riverpod 2.x)AsyncNotifier rentEntriesProvider managing optimistic CRUD, computed summaryProvider for financial metrics, and filteredEntries for instant multi-predicate queries.
Domain Models & Business EntitiesImmutable RentEntry models with Equatable and JSON serialization; calculateStatus() temporal method; PaymentStatus (Paid, Pending, Overdue) enums.
Infrastructure & Supabase Data LayerPostgreSQL Realtime/REST client with Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcing strict tenant isolation (auth.uid() = user_id).

Key Engineering Highlights & Technical Decisions

Temporal Status Calculation Engine

Problem: In traditional databases, storing a static "overdue" flag requires recurring midnight cron jobs.
Solution: In RentEntry.calculateStatus(), status is dynamically evaluated against DateTime.now(). If unpaid and after due date, it becomes overdue instantly with zero server cron dependencies.

Dual Property Type Classification

Problem: Commercial units (shops/suites) have different lease dynamics than residential apartments.
Solution: Implemented a clean property classification system supporting both unit types with customized fields without requiring schema-breaking migrations.

Reactive Multi-Filter Stream

Problem: Filtering and searching large ledgers can trigger excessive UI rebuilds.
Solution: Combined full-text search with status predicates (All, Paid, Pending, Overdue) using Riverpod computed providers with sub-millisecond updates.

Zero-Trust Multi-Tenant Database Security

Problem: Multi-tenant financial apps require ironclad data isolation.
Solution: Enforced Supabase PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) with auth.uid() = user_id, ensuring tenants can only access their authorized records.
